Blinky privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Blinky is a reminder app for iPhone and Mac. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Blinky has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (reminder intervals, character and appearance) are saved on your device so they are remembered next time.
  • On iPhone, the app shows a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. Images of your customised character are saved in a folder shared only with the app's own widget.
  • On Mac, the app reads how many seconds it has been since your last keyboard or mouse input, and whether the screen is locked, so it can pause while you're away. Only that number is read, never what you type, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Blinky collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

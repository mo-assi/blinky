Blinky | Eye Rest App

A tiny friend that blinks, so you remember to.

Blinky lives in your iPhone's Dynamic Island and slowly blinks while you use your phone. Inside the app it also reminds you to look into the distance (20-20-20) and to stand up and stretch.

Common questions

I don't see Blinky in the Dynamic Island. Open Blinky once to start a blink session, and check Settings > Blinky > Live Activities is on. Sessions last up to 8 hours; open the app to start a new one.

Does Blinky collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
